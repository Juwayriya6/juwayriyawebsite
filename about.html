Artist Biography

Juwayriya Abdullahi paints dreamscapes where jade fog meets prairie light. Textile motifs and quiet astronomy drift together like silk in water—soft, symbolic, luminous.

Based in Edmonton / St. Albert, she weaves East African pattern language with memory, landscape, and surreal portraiture.

Statement

I look for the hush between worlds. Threads, constellations, and water‑light guide the way. The palette leans jade, mint, and pearl: tender and otherworldly.
