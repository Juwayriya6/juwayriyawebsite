Artist Biography

Juwayriya Abdullahi is a surrealism artist weaving East African pattern language, mythology, and memory into prairie light. Her paintings layer luminous, gem‑tone palettes with quiet symbolism—gold‑leaf glints, constellations, and textile geometries— to explore presence, erasure, and belonging.

Based in Edmonton / St. Albert, she is a resident artist and arts administrator, continually building community through exhibitions, teaching, and collaborative projects.

Statement

I paint how stories survive erasure. I map memory with cloth‑like grids, celestial diagrams, and figures that slip between worlds. The work is surreal yet grounded—an index of lived histories and imagined futures.
