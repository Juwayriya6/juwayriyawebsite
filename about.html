Artist Biography

Juwayriya Abdullahi paints dreamscapes where soft sky and rose light meet quiet symbols. East African pattern language and prairie atmosphere weave into luminous narratives.

Statement

I look for the hush between worlds—threads, constellations, and water‑light. The palette leans sky, lilac, and peach: tender, elegant, and otherworldly.
