surrealism • mythology • jade‑tone dreams

Artist Biography

Juwayriya Abdullahi paints with mist and memory—East African pattern language, prairie atmospheres, and jade‑bright notes of light. The work is gentle, symbolic, and luminous.

Statement

I look for the hush between worlds. Threads, constellations, and water‑light guide the way.
