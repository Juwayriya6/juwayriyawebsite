About

Juwayriya Abdullahi is a surrealism artist based in Edmonton/St. Albert. Her work draws on ancient histories, East African textiles, and prairie light to explore identity, culture, and social memory.

She is a resident artist and program director at VASA St. Albert, creating community‑oriented programming while developing new bodies of work.
